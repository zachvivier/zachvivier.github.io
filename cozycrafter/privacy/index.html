Cozy Crafter privacy policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Cozy Crafter keeps your projects on your iPhone. There are no Cozy Crafter accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and the app doesn't connect to the internet on its own. This policy explains what that means.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored in Cozy Crafter's storage on your iPhone: crafts, projects, supplies, checklists, notes, photos, shopping items, and settings. None of it is sent to the developer or anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Cozy Crafter doesn't sync between devices; if that changes, this policy will be updated first.

If you choose a photo for a project, a craft icon, or a theme, Cozy Crafter saves a resized copy of only that photo. It cannot see the rest of your photo library.

Links you add

A shop link you add to a supply opens in Safari when you tap it. Cozy Crafter doesn't visit the link itself.

Beta testing with TestFlight

If you test a beta version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares some information with the developer: the name and email address you were invited with, your device model and iOS version, install and session counts, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. This information is used only to fix problems and improve Cozy Crafter. Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy also apply.

Children

Cozy Crafter does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
