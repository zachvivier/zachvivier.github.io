Cozy Crafter support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email zachvivierdev@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

Common questions

How do I join the beta?

Email zachvivierdev@gmail.com with the email address of your Apple Account. You'll get a TestFlight invite. Install Apple's free TestFlight app, open the invite on your iPhone, and install Cozy Crafter from TestFlight.

How do I send beta feedback?

Take a screenshot in Cozy Crafter and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open TestFlight, choose Cozy Crafter, and tap Send Beta Feedback. Email works too.

How do I add my own craft?

Open the Crafts tab and tap +, or tap New craft at the end of the craft list when you start a new project. Give it a name and pick a sticker, an emoji, or one of your own photos as its icon.

What do Making, Dreaming, Napping, and Finished mean?

Making is a project you're working on now. Dreaming is one you plan to start. Napping is one that's on hold. Finished is done, with confetti.

How does the Shopping tab work?

It lists every supply you still need to buy, from every project that isn't Finished. Tap + to add something that isn't for a particular project. An item can have an amount, a link to the shop, and notes. Mark an item as bought when you have it.

Does Cozy Crafter sync between devices?

Not yet. Your projects are stored only on your iPhone.

Is my data backed up?

Your projects are stored only on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes them. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a computer, that backup includes Cozy Crafter.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe left on a project to delete it, along with its supplies, checklist, and notes. A craft you delete keeps its projects. To remove everything, delete the app. See the privacy policy for details.
