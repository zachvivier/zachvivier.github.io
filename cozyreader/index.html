Cozy Reader

Cozy Reader is a web app for keeping track of your reading. Log a book in print, ebook, or audio, with its rating, notes, genres, and dates. Make lists by hand or by rules, set a goal for the year, and watch your stats. A reading timer records how long you read each book, and three pixel-art cats walk along the top of the screen.

Where it runs

Cozy Reader began as an iPhone app on TestFlight. It is no longer there. It now runs as a web app at home on my Tailnet, saved to the iPhone Home Screen. Here is why I moved it.

If you would like to run it yourself, email zachvivierdev@gmail.com. I can share the source on GitHub.

How Cozy Reader was built

Book details and covers come from Open Library, a project of the Internet Archive, and from other public book catalogs. Cozy Reader is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of them.
