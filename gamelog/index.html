Gamelog

Gamelog is an iPhone app for keeping track of the games you play: ratings, notes, where and when you played, a wishlist, and patch notes for your Steam games.

Join the beta

Gamelog is in beta on TestFlight, Apple's app for testing apps before they reach the App Store. To try it, email zachvivierdev@gmail.com with the email address of your Apple Account, and you'll get an invite. You'll need an iPhone and the free TestFlight app.
